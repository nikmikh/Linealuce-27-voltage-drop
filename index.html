About

I made this tool to check Linealuce 27 Full Remote circuits in minutes, without a spreadsheet for every project.

Place power supplies, drivers and luminaires, wire them together and the page calculates the voltage at every luminaire, the drop in each cable, the allowed cable lengths and cross-sections, and checks power supply and driver loads against the iGuzzini instruction sheets for the Mono, Tunable White and RGBW versions.

Nothing leaves your browser. The page loads one font stylesheet and sends nothing. Schemes stay in memory until you save them as a file, and files you open are read locally.
